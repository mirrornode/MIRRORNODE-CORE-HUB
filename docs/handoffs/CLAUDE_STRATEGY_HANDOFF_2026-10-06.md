## Established current state

### 1. MIRRORNODE production identity

**OBSERVED:** `mirrornode.xyz` is the canonical production domain and public front door. It is attached in authenticated Vercel state to `mirrornode-platform`.

**OBSERVED:** `api.mirrornode.xyz` is attached to `mirrornode-backend`.

**OBSERVED:** `parallax.mirrornode.xyz` exists as an authenticated Vercel alias attached to the Vercel project currently named `public`.

### 2. Controlled domains

**OBSERVED:** MIRRORNODE controls two presently evidenced registered domains relevant to this decision:

- `mirrornode.xyz`
- `mirrornodeconsulting.com`

The February 2026 GoDaddy order trail establishes purchase of both.

**UNKNOWN / unverified:** `mirrornode.com` must not be treated as controlled. One historical banking-introduction image used `contact@mirrornode.com`; that contact identity is stale/unverified and is being preserved as historical evidence rather than reused.

### 3. Business email

**UNKNOWN:** no working domain-based MIRRORNODE mailbox on `.xyz` or `mirrornodeconsulting.com` has yet been established by the current evidence pass.

**OBSERVED:** Atlassian continues to report that its requested DNS records for email-domain verification of `mirrornode.xyz` do not yet match. This does not negate the working Vercel web-domain attachment.

### 4. Parallax

Historical MIRRORNODE system maps describe Parallax as the public communication / public narrative / publishing / outward-facing material layer.

The current deployment evidence proves the alias exists, but does not by itself establish what Parallax should be now.

### 5. House RAMESES / House Ramses

**PROPOSED:** House RAMESES is a house-level identity above individual programs, envisioned as a human-facing home for orientation, gathering, invitation, collaboration, and possibly portable companion/context experiences.

It is not currently established as a governance authority, production application, membership body, or public institution.

### 6. Matchmaker

**PROPOSED / working draft:** Matchmaker is an invitation-only external challenge/review layer. Its core principles are artifact-before-community, challenge-before-endorsement, granular consent, non-decisional external input, visible disposition of criticism, and no implied authority or membership.

This creates an important design question: Matchmaker and House RAMESES may be adjacent, but they should not accidentally collapse reviewer independence into community membership or community participation into review authority.

### 7. Private/control surfaces

MOPCON / Coordination Office and related operator surfaces should remain conceptually distinct from public community or publishing surfaces. Private operational truth must not leak merely because a public experience would be easier to build by reusing the same surface.

## Specific questions we want challenged

### Parallax

1. Does Parallax still deserve to exist as a distinct public surface?
2. If yes, what is the one job it should do that `mirrornode.xyz` should not?
3. Should Parallax become:
   - MIRRORNODE's publication/research journal,
   - a visual/storytelling layer,
   - House RAMESES' outward-facing publication wing,
   - a sandbox for public experiments,
   - a services presence,
   - or something else?
4. What would justify retiring it or folding it into the main platform?
5. What should never be published there?

### House RAMESES

1. Is “House” a useful organizing metaphor or does it create unnecessary ambiguity for customers and collaborators?
2. Should RAMESES be primarily:
   - a community home,
   - an orientation/onboarding layer,
   - a gathering/events identity,
   - a membership layer,
   - a publication house,
   - a companion application,
   - an umbrella over selected human-facing programs,
   - or a deliberately small combination of these?
3. What is the smallest version worth launching?
4. What governance, moderation, privacy, consent, accessibility, and safety boundaries would be required before inviting real people in?
5. How can it feel like “home” without becoming a vague catch-all for every MIRRORNODE idea?
6. Should the name be styled `RAMESES`, “House RAMESES,” “House Ramses,” or something else for public legibility?

### The two controlled domains

1. Is the cleanest model:
   - `.xyz` = technical/product/public platform and `mirrornodeconsulting.com` = legal/business/email identity;
   - `.xyz` = everything public, consulting domain = redirect/protective asset;
   - consulting domain = conventional corporate front door and `.xyz` = product/lab ecosystem;
   - or another split?
2. Would using two visible domains increase trust or simply fragment identity?
3. Which domain should carry official business email, and why?
4. If one domain redirects to the other, which direction makes the most sense over a 3–5 year horizon?
5. What domain architecture best survives adding future products without another identity migration?
