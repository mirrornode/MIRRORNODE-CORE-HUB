## Strategic design questions now open

1. **Parallax:** what job should it uniquely do that the main platform should not?
2. **House RAMESES:** is it a place, a program, an umbrella identity, a community operating layer, a companion application, or a combination with explicit sub-boundaries?
3. **Domain split:** should `.xyz` remain the product/technical home while `mirrornodeconsulting.com` becomes the legal/business/email identity, or would that create unnecessary identity fragmentation?
4. **Email:** which controlled domain should carry official business email, and what mailbox/admin/recovery arrangement is required before publication?
5. **Publishing:** where should long-form public explanation, research, field notes, governance methods, and review summaries live — main platform, Parallax, House RAMESES, or a deliberately tiered model?
6. **Community versus review:** how should House RAMESES relate to Matchmaker without turning reviewers into members, members into reviewers, or either group into implied authorities?
7. **Physical / virtual gatherings:** if House RAMESES supports real-world and virtual gathering, what admission, moderation, privacy, accessibility, and safety boundaries are needed before launch?
8. **Portable context:** should a House RAMESES companion experience exist as a lightweight context/task/music/orientation surface distinct from MOPCON and private operator systems?
9. **Brand clarity:** can a new person explain in one minute the difference among MIRRORNODE, Osiris, Parallax, House RAMESES, Matchmaker, MOPCON, and the consulting/legal entity?
10. **Reduction test:** which surfaces can be combined or retired without losing a genuinely distinct function?

## Decision criteria for future recommendations

Any proposed architecture should be judged against:

- clarity to a first-time visitor;
- preservation of Operator and governance boundaries;
- separation of public projection from private operational truth;
- low maintenance burden for a founder-operated system;
- commercial usefulness and customer trust;
- ability to preserve evidence and correction history;
- privacy and consent for community/reviewer participation;
- graceful future expansion without requiring a premature platform rebuild;
- domain/email recoverability and administrative custody;
- whether each named surface has a unique, defensible job.

## Current preferred non-binding planning frame

This is a **PROPOSED** working frame for comparison, not a decision:

- `mirrornode.xyz` — canonical technical/product/public front door.
- `mirrornodeconsulting.com` — business/legal/contact identity, possibly redirecting to `.xyz` for public web traffic while supporting verified business email.
- Parallax — publication/storytelling/explanation layer if it can justify a distinct role.
- House RAMESES — human/community/orientation/gathering umbrella, not the execution or governance control plane.
- Matchmaker — bounded review program with its own consent and independence rules, potentially introduced through House RAMESES but not governed by community popularity.
- MOPCON / Coordination Office — private or controlled operating/coordination surfaces, not public community spaces.

This frame should be challenged before implementation.
