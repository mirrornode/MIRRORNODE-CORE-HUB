## Normalized doctrinal acknowledgements

The external acknowledgements recognize the principal locked safeguards:

1. **Canonical boundary** — CORE-HUB is the MIRRORNODE canonical-governance promotion/authority plane; legal/issued and executed client records retain authority in their own subject matter.
2. **Immutable external anchors** — mutable branches, moving paths, `latest` aliases, or mutable URLs are insufficient canonical anchors; external anchors require immutable revision and reproducible hash metadata.
3. **Role separation** — decision, approval, execution authorization, execution, and verification remain distinct authority roles.
4. **Emergency control** — emergency authority cannot be self-created or enlarged and continuation requires pre-existing source-backed human authority.
5. **Legal-hold protection** — hold-release authorization and deletion execution are separated.
6. **Fail-closed freshness** — missing, expired, unavailable, or untrusted review/time state cannot remain implicitly current for consequential use.
7. **Schema identity** — `record_id` is the sole durable structured-record identifier; domain-specific IDs are aliases only.
