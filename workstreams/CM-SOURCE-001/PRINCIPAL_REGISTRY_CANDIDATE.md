## Required fields for final registry

Each principal record MUST eventually include:

- stable `principal_id`;
- human-readable name;
- role and responsibilities;
- authenticated service identities;
- effective capabilities;
- explicit authority envelope;
- prohibited actions / escalation triggers;
- connected systems and scope boundaries;
- evidence references;
- trust/reliability history where operationally relevant;
- last verified time;
- verification method;
- current matter assignments;
- recent action receipts;
- expiry/review date where permissions are temporary.
