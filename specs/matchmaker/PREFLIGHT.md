# Matchmaker Preflight

Status: preflight  
Lane: Resonance Matchmaker  
Repository: MIRRORNODE-CORE-HUB  
Authority: Operator-reviewed governance spec  
Implementation: not authorized in this document

## Purpose

Matchmaker is the Phase 2 lead workstream for translating human, team, and system needs into coherent alignment paths.

Its first responsibility is not automation. Its first responsibility is discernment: identify what kind of match is being requested, what boundaries apply, what evidence is available, and what should not be inferred.

## Scope

Matchmaker may evaluate:

- stated goals
- role fit
- project/workstream fit
- collaboration compatibility
- service/offering alignment
- operational readiness
- declared preferences and constraints

Matchmaker must not infer hidden psychological, spiritual, medical, legal, political, or intimate attributes from weak signals.

## First Use Cases

1. Match a person or team to the right Mirrornode offer.
2. Match a project to the correct workstream or repository.
3. Match a system problem to the correct node or review lane.
4. Match a collaborator to the right level of access, responsibility, or covenant.
5. Match a customer need to Osiris Audit, Mirror Mirror, Fox Story Game, or later services.

## Non-Goals

Matchmaker is not:

- a dating engine
- a surveillance engine
- a personality diagnosis tool
- a coercive ranking system
- a hidden scoring system
- an authority override mechanism

Matchmaker cannot replace Operator judgment, security review, or covenant-level consent.

## Required Inputs

Every Matchmaker run must declare:

- request source
- matching objective
- available evidence
- explicit constraints
- disallowed inference zones
- recommended output type
- review authority

## Output Shape

A Matchmaker output should include:

- match category
- confidence level
- supporting evidence
- disqualifying concerns
- missing information
- recommended next step
- review gate

## Governance Gates

Before implementation, the following must be approved:

1. Data boundary: what Matchmaker may read.
2. Consent boundary: who authorized the match request.
3. Output boundary: what Matchmaker is allowed to say.
4. Storage boundary: what may be retained.
5. Escalation boundary: what requires Theia, THOTH, Operator, or security review.
6. Product boundary: which public-facing claims are allowed.

## Phase 0 Acceptance Criteria

- this spec is committed through PR
