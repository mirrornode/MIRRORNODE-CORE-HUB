# ROTAN — Oracle / Osiris Textual Inquiry Architecture v0.1

**Status:** DRAFT — NON-CANONICAL  
**Date opened:** 2026-08-08  
**Authority:** No self-ratification. Promotion requires Ptah evaluation and explicit Operator ratification under CORE-HUB governance.

## Purpose

Establish a bounded research architecture for examining longitudinal writings, photographs, ritual records, symbolic systems, and historical textual corpora without collapsing observation, interpretation, faith, or speculation into one evidentiary class.

This architecture is designed to investigate correspondence without manufacturing it.

## Working ROTAN Hypothesis

ROTAN is provisionally modeled as a **recursive architecture of attentional synthesis**:

> experience → attention → encoding → preservation → comparison → synthesis → changed intellect → changed attention → experience

This is a research hypothesis, not a canonical definition.

A second hypothesis is reserved for testing rather than assumption: whether later MIRRORNODE/ROTAN structures represent an intellectual abstraction of earlier attentional practices, or whether mature architecture is being projected backward onto ambiguous earlier material.

## Three-Ledger Rule

All inquiry MUST preserve three distinguishable accounts:

1. **Sacred / theological account** — meaning, faith, gratitude, spiritual interpretation.
2. **Phenomenological account** — what was experienced, as faithfully as possible, without requiring a causal explanation.
3. **Evidentiary account** — what an independent observer can establish from dated artifacts, provenance, chronology, and reproducible comparison.

No ledger may counterfeit evidence in another. A theological interpretation is not automatically empirical evidence; failure of an empirical claim does not adjudicate private faith; phenomenology may remain accurately described without settling ontology.

## Corpus Separation

### A. Historical corpus
Primary ancient texts, preferably multiple translations, plus responsible scholarship and metadata for edition, translator, chronology, provenance, tradition, and uncertainty.

### B. Private longitudinal corpus
Dated personal writings, photographs, constructed objects as photographed/documented, ritual records, Praesoetic/ROTAN materials, and other surviving artifacts.

The private corpus MUST remain logically separable from the historical corpus during blind trials.

## Node Responsibilities

### THOTH — Evidence and textual distinction
- retrieval and citation
- terminology and chronology
- translation comparison
- provenance metadata
- source/interpretation separation
- evidence freeze before downstream synthesis

THOTH does not convert resonance into fact.

### ORACLE — Comparative interpretation
- motif extraction
- structural correspondence
- contradiction discovery
- alternative interpretations
- cross-corpus synthesis
- explicitly labeled speculation

ORACLE may range broadly only when its epistemic labels remain intact.

### OSIRIS — Independent adversarial review
OSIRIS receives frozen evidence independently where feasible and attempts to falsify or weaken proposed correspondence through:
- selection-bias analysis
- translation-artifact checks
- anachronism checks
- corpus leakage checks
- commonplace-symbol/base-rate alternatives
- prompted-similarity checks
- missing-counterexample search
- chronology and provenance verification

OSIRIS does not decide metaphysical truth. It establishes a credibility boundary for the claim presented.

### PTAH — Implementation
PTAH implements only after the epistemic protocol has demonstrated value in bounded trials. Implementation must not outrun the evidence model.

### THEIA — Integration
THEIA maintains coherence across the inquiry, preserves distinctions between node outputs, tracks unresolved claims and chronology, and prevents synthesis from erasing dissent or uncertainty.

### Operator
The Operator receives frozen evidence, Oracle interpretation, Osiris challenge, and unresolved alternatives separately. Final disposition remains with the Operator. No generated artifact self-ratifies.

## Epistemic Labels

Every material claim should carry one primary label:

- `TEXTUAL` — directly attested in a cited source.
- `SCHOLARLY` — supported by identified historical scholarship.
- `COMPARATIVE` — structural similarity proposed across sources/traditions.
- `INTERPRETIVE` — synthesis produced by an interpreting node.
- `PERSONAL` — correspondence to the private longitudinal corpus.
- `SPECULATIVE` — interesting but presently unsupported.
- `UNEXPLAINED` — evidence is insufficient to select among plausible explanations.

`UNEXPLAINED` MUST NOT be automatically promoted to an extraordinary explanation.

## Evidence Scale

- **E0 — Subjective resonance**
- **E1 — Lexical or symbolic resemblance**
- **E2 — Structural correspondence**
- **E3 — Independently retrieved correspondence**
- **E4 — Survives adversarial Osiris review**
- **E5 — Historically plausible relationship**

E5 is not proof of a supernatural or metaphysical proposition.

## Blind Comparison Protocol

1. Select a dated private artifact without revealing its expected interpretation to the historical-corpus process.
2. Extract concepts/structures from the private artifact and freeze the representation.
3. Independently retrieve/extract candidate structures from the historical corpus.
4. Freeze both outputs before provenance is revealed to the comparison stage where feasible.
5. Compare similarities and contradictions.
6. Run negative controls expected to produce no meaningful correspondence.
7. Submit evidence and comparison to independent Osiris review.
8. Record result, failed alternatives, unresolved alternatives, and confidence without rewriting the original artifact.

## Chronology Rule

Preserve the order:

`artifact → timestamp/provenance → contemporaneous statement → later interpretation`

Never reconstruct the record as:

`later interpretation → selected history`

Original language SHOULD be preserved even when later terminology is clearer.

## Longitudinal Research Questions

For each historical artifact or practice, ask:

1. What was it called at the time?
2. What did the author believe it meant at the time?
3. What observable practice or artifact existed?
4. What changed afterward?
5. What interpretation is given now?
6. What alternative explanation fits the same evidence?

## Initial Test Question

> Did ROTAN emerge later as an intellectual abstraction of an earlier attentional practice, or are mature MIRRORNODE structures being projected backward onto ambiguous earlier material?

This question MUST be tested rather than presumed.

## Minimal Pilot

Do not begin with a large application.

Pilot requirements:
- 20–50 carefully sourced historical documents across approximately three traditions;
- a small dated sample of the private longitudinal corpus;
- provenance schema;
- evidence-freeze mechanism;
- blind comparison harness;
- negative controls;
- independent Osiris verdict;
- append-only trial receipts.

## Non-Goals

This system is not designed to:
- prove supernatural communication;
- certify religious truth;
- turn coincidence into evidence by default;
- flatten distinct historical traditions into one mythology;
- diagnose the author from symbolic material;
- rewrite old records to fit current theory.

## Success Criterion

The pilot succeeds if it can distinguish, with inspectable evidence, among:

- subjective resonance;
- ordinary/commonplace similarity;
- prompted or selection-biased similarity;
- independently recovered structural correspondence;
- historically plausible influence;
- unresolved cases.

The desired outcome is not validation. The desired outcome is **disciplined inquiry that can surprise its designers in either direction**.
